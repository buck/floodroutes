Houston Flood Routes

A hackathon demonstration of flood-aware driving directions for Houston.

Not for emergency use. This map shows where streets have flooded in the past. It knows nothing about today's water. A street with no history can still flood. During active flooding, follow official guidance, never drive into water of unknown depth, and call 911 if you are trapped. Turn around, don't drown.

What it does

For any trip it shows two routes: the normal fastest route (grey) and a flood-aware route (blue) that steers around streets with a history of flooding. The flood-aware route only prefers other streets; it never closes a road. On a typical trip the detour costs about half a minute.

Where the flood history comes from

  • City of Houston 311 service requests, 2011–2026. About 163,000 reports of flooding, standing water and drainage problems, grouped into ~130 m hexagons. The score weights repeat flooding across separate storms, so a street that floods in storm after storm scores higher than one with many calls in a single storm. Older reports count less (the city does fix drainage).
  • Houston firefighters. Street-level comments from the 2018 survey of all 88 HFD stations in Measuring Flooding with the Houston Fire Department (Perry & Stein, Rice University / Kinder Institute, 2019), shown as the purple "Firefighter reports" layer.

Known limits

  • 311 records where people report, not where water is. Roads with few homes beside them (bayou-side boulevards, freeway frontage roads, underpasses) are under-reported. So are neighbourhoods where fewer residents call 311.
  • History, not live conditions. No rain, gauge or road-sensor data is used yet.
  • Addresses are approximate. Most are placed along their block from Census address ranges.

Official, live information

  • Houston TranStar: roadway flooding
  • Harris County Flood Warning System (rainfall and bayou levels)
  • ReadyHarris (Harris County emergency information)
  • National Weather Service, Houston/Galveston

Privacy

No resident names, comments or case numbers are on this site; only aggregate history. Addresses you type and your location are used to answer your request and are not stored: the server logs page paths, not the addresses or coordinates in a request. Map tiles are cached on this server, so your browser does not contact OpenStreetMap directly.
